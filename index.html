About Us

VTC Cha Ladka Shree Ganpati Mandal is one of Mumbai's most prestigious and devotional organizations. Blending social responsibility, Maharashtrian culture, and deep devotion, we celebrate the grand festival of Lord Ganesha every year. Through Bappa's blessings, we organize annual blood donation drives, educational distribution programs, and vibrant cultural events for the community.
